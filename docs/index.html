Skim Detector Firmware

Vord C5

Continuous BLE skimmer detection for ESP32-C5 boards, always-on skimmer hunting.

Detection only. Vord C5 passively listens for and flags suspicious BLE signatures — An educational tool: a match is a signal to be cautious, not proof.

What to do if you find a skimmer

        
Do not touch it. Do not tamper with it. Call the authorities.

        
A skimmer is evidence in a crime. Handling it can destroy evidence, may be illegal, and can be dangerous — these devices are installed by criminals.

        
    
          
  1. Do not touch or remove anything. Leave the device, the pump/terminal, and the area exactly as you found it.
    2. 
          
  2. Do not pay or insert your card there. Use a different terminal, pay inside, or use contactless/cash.
    3. 
          
  3. Note the location and pump/terminal number — and, if it's safe, the dashboard details (device name, MAC, time). Don't linger or confront anyone.
    4. 
          
  4. Tell the on-site staff so they can shut the terminal down — but staff are not a substitute for the police.
    5. 
          
  5. Call the authorities. Report it to your local police (non-emergency line), or the emergency number if you feel unsafe.
            
      
              
    • In the US you can also report to the FBI, the FTC, and your card issuer.
      • 
            
    
          
    6. 
          
  6. If you used a card there recently, monitor your statements and alert your bank.
    7. 
        

        
⚠️ Vord C5 is an educational detection aid, not a law-enforcement tool. A match is a signal to be cautious, not proof — investigation, removal, and evidence handling must be left to the police.
